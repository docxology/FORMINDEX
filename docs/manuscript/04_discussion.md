# Discussion

Topic subsets are retrieval aids whose precision depends on the fields and
matching rule. A topic audit can compare title and keyword matches separately,
inspect ambiguous cases and record the search rule beside the results.
Any future selector change needs to account for outputs made by the
current rule.

Publication and venue plots describe a given export. A location field may
refer to a publication address, while a co-authorship visualization depends
on the spelling of author names in the records. Interpreting charts across
time, languages or geographies requires checking the source fields and
coverage limits documented in the original report [@friedman2024oct13].

The fixture was retained to exercise ingestion. It supports conclusions
about this sample and the script behavior. Estimating topical prevalence
in ant research would require a defined sampling design and relevance
judgments. This check covers neither the full export nor the historical
provider-generated interpretations.
